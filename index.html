Life After School

Navigating the next chapter after graduation can feel overwhelming. Here are key areas to focus on:

🎓 Career & Study

Whether university, trade apprenticeship, or entering the workforce, explore options that match your passions—not just expectations.

💰 Financial Basics

Learn budgeting basics early. Track income, manage student loans, and set up a small emergency savings pot.

🧘 Managing Burnout

Taking a gap year or working part-time is okay. Life isn't a race—build habits that prioritize your wellbeing.

🤝 Building Community

Leaving school means friend groups change. Join clubs, volunteer, or take up sports to stay connected with new people.

Get Help Now

If you or someone you know needs immediate assistance, contact these confidential services:

  • 🚨 Emergency Services: Call 111 (NZ) / 911 (US) / 000 (AU) For immediate danger or life-threatening situations.
  • 📞 Need to Talk? Call or Text 1737 Free, confidential 24/7 support with trained counselors.
  • 💬 Youthline: Call 0800 376 633 or Text 234 Dedicated youth-focused support service (8am - midnight).
  • 🌈 Rainbow Youth: (09) 376 4155 Support for LGBTQIA+ youth and their families.

Privacy Conditions

Your Safety & Data Protection

The Lighthouse Project values your trust and privacy.

  • Anonymity: Browsing this website does not store personal identification or medical data.
  • External Links: Clicking helpline numbers or crisis text options routes directly through your device's native phone or messaging app.
  • Confidentiality: Calls or texts made to helpline numbers (e.g., 1737 or Youthline) are private between you and the respective care provider.
